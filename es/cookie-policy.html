1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

TopMusicianGear uses essential cookies for basic functionality, Google Analytics cookies to understand site traffic, and affiliate tracking cookies for commission tracking. When you click on affiliate links, partner retailers may also set their own cookies. By continuing to use this site, you consent to the use of these cookies. You can manage your preferences via our cookie consent banner.
