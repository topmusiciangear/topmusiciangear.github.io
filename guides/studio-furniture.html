Your studio environment affects your work more than you think. Bad ergonomics lead to fatigue and bad decisions.

How to Choose the Best Studio Furniture?

Your studio furniture is the foundation of your workflow. A proper desk, monitor stands, and cable management can make the difference between a productive session and a frustrating one.

Desk height and ergonomics matter. Your ears should be at the same height as your monitor tweeters. Your arms should form a 90-degree angle at the keyboard. Investing in an adjustable desk or proper chair can save you from years of back and neck pain.

Cable management is not optional. A clean studio is a productive studio. Use cable trays, velcro ties, and desk grommets to keep everything organized. It takes an afternoon to set up and saves you hours of troubleshooting later.
