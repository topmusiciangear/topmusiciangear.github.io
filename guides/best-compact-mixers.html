Best Rack-Mount Digital Mixers

Rack mixers put full digital consoles where faders cannot go: in-ear rigs, fly-racks, install closets and broadcast trucks. These are the rack-mount digital mixers that crews actually tour with — from the 1U Midas brain to the 48-channel WING flagship.

By Daniel Carnago · August 2026

Touring and session musician with 20+ years on stages around the world. A great deal of the gear I recommend here I have used myself, on tours, in professional studios, or in my own studio.

Verdict
X32 Rack for IEM rigs and community support; Ui24R for app-free browser mixing and USB drive/DAW recording; WING Rack for 48-channel flagship power; SQ-Rack for 96kHz rack power without faders; XR18 for budget small-venue bands; M32C for 1U fly-rigs; CQ-20B for fast automated mixing; StudioLive SE 24R for Studio One studios.
Behringer X32 Rack
Pros
  • Massive global user community with shared presets and tutorials
  • AES50 expansion over cheap CAT5 stagebox cabling
  • Up to 14 separate monitor mixes for in-ear rigs
  • 32x32 USB plus Ultranet for P-16 personal monitoring
Cons
  • 48kHz-only processing with no high-res path
  • Official remote app feels dated next to newer rivals
  • No motorized faders — all mixing via app, editor or MIDI
  • 3U chassis plus tablet and router per gig

Cons

  • No physical screen or fallback controls if the network drops
  • No motorized faders — a connected device is mandatory
  • Dual-band Wi-Fi still needs care in crowded RF venues

How to Choose the Best Rack-Mount Digital Mixer?

Rack mixers trade faders for remote control — pick by inputs, not knobs. Count local mic preamps first: stagebox units like the M32C and CQ-20B put processing in the rack and mixing on your tablet, while rack consoles like the SQ-Rack keep a touchscreen for engineers who mix by feel. Check recording next: USB multitrack, SD backup, or full DAW integration through AES50, AVB or Dante stageboxes. Wireless control matters on stage — browser mixing needs no apps while touchscreen models keep working when tablets die. Made for bands, venues and engineers going rack; check input count, network ecosystem and backup recording before buying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Almost always. Only the Ui24R works from any browser and the WING Rack and SQ-Rack carry touchscreens — every other rack mixer here needs a connected phone, tablet or computer. Bring a backup device and your own router to every gig.
Most of them can. The Ui24R records multitrack to a USB drive **or** acts as a USB interface for a DAW — not both simultaneously. The WING Rack and CQ-20B record multitrack to SD plus USB. The SQ-Rack adds multitrack SD backup to USB. The StudioLive 24R records stereo (L/R) to SD only, not multitrack. The XR18, X32 Rack and M32C need a connected computer for multitrack recording.
They are three ecosystems that do not mix. The Behringer and Midas racks speak AES50 over cheap CAT5, the StudioLive 24R speaks AVB to Series III consoles, and the SQ range takes Dante, Waves or MADI through an option card. The CQ-20B has no network audio ports at all — it works in complete isolation, not part of any stagebox ecosystem.
More than most bands need. The X32 Rack has 16 assignable mix buses, configurable for up to 16 mono or 8 stereo monitor mixes for IEM systems. The M32C, WING Rack and StudioLive 24R all drive full-band IEM systems with personal mixing over Ultranet, ME or EarMix.
Go rack if you mix from stage or a tablet and travel light — a rack mixer controlled by a tablet eliminates the need to haul heavy analog consoles and cable snakes. Go console like the SQ-5 or X32 Compact if you prefer the feel and control of physical faders.

Conclusion

For IEM systems, the X32 Rack remains the standard with its app ecosystem and CAT5 stagebox expansion. Bands who hate apps should buy the Ui24R for browser mixing and multitrack USB recording (to drive or as DAW interface, not simultaneously). Engineers wanting maximum power get the 48-stereo-channel WING Rack, while 96kHz purists have the faderless SQ-Rack or the self-mixing CQ-20B. Travel light with the 1U M32C plus DL stage boxes, choose the XR18 for the budget option, or bridge studio and stage with the StudioLive SE 24R.
