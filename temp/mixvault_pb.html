Product information

A New Immersive EQ Experience

MIXVAULT analyzes your audio, helps you find a great tonal balance, then lets you shape it by feel, with every move level matched. Pull a frequency closer to bring it forward, push it away to pull it back, and build mixes that sound clearer, fuller, and more balanced, without any transient or phase smearing.

Quick Start:

  1. Load MIXVAULT on any track, bus, or full mix. 
  2. Shape by feel. Pull a frequency towards you to make it louder, push it away to make it quieter, every move perfectly level-matched in real time. 
  3. Want a fast starting point? Hit ANALYZE, pick your source (mix, vocals, bass, drums, or music) and genre, and you'll get a Smart Preset built from your actual audio. 
  4. Step through the preset arrows to explore alternatives, each labelled with its character (dark, balanced, bright), and bypass to A/B against your original.

Features

  • Immersive 3D interface — pull a frequency toward you to boost it, push it away to cut. The room adjusts to give you perspective.
  • Smart Presets — hit Analyze and get a starting point built from your actual audio, by source and genre.
  • Level Match — every move is loudness-matched in real time, so you hear the true tonal change, not just "louder."
  • Fixed, phase-stable bands — boosts and cuts stay clean, so transients stay sharp and the low end stays tight.
  • Live delta visual — watch exactly how your EQ shapes the signal as it plays.
  • Bass mode — BASSROOM-grade low-end filtering that boosts big without going soft.
  • Four resolution modes — Broad, Balanced, Detailed and Bass; switch anytime without losing your work.
  • Reference track import — load a track you trust and shape toward its tonal balance.
  • Zoomable Hertz strips — scroll to zoom any part of the spectrum, or type an exact dB value for fine control.

Common questions

Which plugin can help make my bass sound clearer?
MIXVAULT. Its Bass mode focuses five bands on 20 to 320 Hz, using filters with minimal phase and transient distortion, so you can add weight while the kick still hits.

What's a good EQ plugin for mixing music fast?
MIXVAULT builds Smart Presets from your own audio and level matches every move, so you always hear the true tonal change rather than a louder version.

  • Apple Silicon Native.
