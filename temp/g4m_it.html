Pure sonic power unmatched by other compact monitors

Compact yet powerful, the Eris Studio 5 is here to revolutionize your studio monitoring experience. Equipped with a 5.25-inch woven-composite low-frequency transducer, these monitor deliver tight and clear bass with an impressive punch. Don't be fooled by its small size – the Eris Studio 5's bass response reaches down to an astonishing 48 Hz, making it perfect for any mixing space, whether it's a home studio or a small professional mix room.

Experience pristine high-frequency details with the ultra-low-mass silk-dome high-frequency transducer. The custom-designed EBM waveguide, with a 100-degree (horizontal) and 60-degree (vertical) dispersion, ensures a wide sweet spot and lifelike stereo imaging for an immersive listening experience. You'll be able to hear the finest nuances in your mix, thanks to the superior high-frequency response provided by the EBM waveguide.

Cutting-edge design - optimised to deliver high-end acoustic performance

Say goodbye to unwanted resonances and reflections with the Eris Studio 5's front-firing acoustic port and optimized internal bracing. The ported enclosure enhances bass-frequency reproduction, giving your mix that extra depth and richness. Additionally, the narrow vertical dispersion reduces reflections from your desk, allowing for a more accurate monitoring environment.

With 80W of Class AB bi-amplification, the Eris Studio 5 delivers ample power to handle your mixing needs. The bi-amped design ensures that each driver is independently powered, resulting in enhanced clarity and accuracy across the frequency range. From the low-end bass to the detailed highs, this monitor delivers a balanced and precise sound representation.

Onboard controls - adapt your monitor to any space

Fine-tune your monitoring setup with the Eris Studio 5's acoustic tuning controls. Adjust the high-frequency response with the continuously variable HF control, and shape the midrange frequencies with the continuously variable Midrange control. The Acoustic Space control allows you to compensate for any acoustic anomalies in your room, offering flat, -2dB, or -4dB settings. Additionally, the highpass filter can be set to Off, 80 Hz, or 100 Hz, providing further control over your low-frequency response.

Robust construction with power saving mode and more

Rest assured that the Eris Studio 5 is built to last and protect your investment. With features such as RF interference protection, output-current limiting, over-temperature protection, transient protection, subsonic protection, and amplifier "soft start" feature, these monitor are designed to handle any situation and ensure reliable performance. The internal power supply with an IEC connector and power switch with an LED indicator further adds to the convenience and ease of use.

Connectivity is a breeze with the Eris Studio 5. It features balanced XLR and 1/4-inch inputs, as well as unbalanced RCA inputs, allowing you to connect it to a wide range of audio sources. Whether you're using professional studio equipment or consumer-grade devices, the Eris Studio 5 has you covered.
