01

Use On Channels Or Full Mix

MIXVAULT works on individual tracks, busses, and the master. Four Resolution options set how many bands you get, from wide and sweeping to tight and surgical, so the same EQ handles a quick vocal tilt and a careful mastering move without ever changing how it sounds.

02

Shape By Feel

The visual is something you can reach out and grab. Pull a frequency towards you to make it louder, push it away to make it quieter, and the whole room expands and contracts as you work. Scroll to adjust the Q. You don't just hear your changes, you watch your sound take shape. It's the first EQ that feels less like a tool and more like an instrument.

03

Smart Presets

Hit Analyze, pick your source (mix, vocals, bass, drums, or music) and genre, and MIXVAULT builds a preset from your actual audio, not a stored curve. Step through the arrows to explore alternatives, each labelled by character from bass heavy and dark through to bright and piercing. You'll usually know within four or five clicks.

04

Level Matched

Humans hear louder as better, so any EQ move that nudges the level fools you into thinking you improved the sound. MIXVAULT level matches every move in real time. Audition presets, bypass to compare, push a band, and you're always hearing the true tonal change and nothing else.

05

Broad, Balanced, Detailed

Broad gives you 4 wide bands for sweeping tonal moves. Balanced gives you 6 for general mastering, the one you'll reach for most. Detailed gives you 9 tighter bands when there's a specific problem area to target. Switch at any time and MIXVAULT carries your tonal intent across rather than starting over.

06

Bass

Five bands focused from 20 to 320 Hz, for dialling in bass and sub with everything else out of the way. It runs an improved version of the filter we built for BASSROOM, with minimal phase and transient distortion. Push 240 Hz up 6dB and the weight goes up while the kick still hits like a kick.

07

Fast, and Transparent

MIXVAULT default filters delivers the exact frequency response of a market-leading linear-phase EQ, at its maximum setting - with 36x less latency.

08

Reference Tracks

Your ears adjust to whatever you've been listening to, which is why a mix can sound perfect after an hour and wrong the next morning. Import a professional release you trust, analyze, and MIXVAULT shows you the moves that get your track into the same pocket. Perfectly level matched, so you're hearing tone and not just a louder record.

09

See Every Change

As your audio plays, a live delta visual paints onto the sides of the room, so you can see exactly how your EQ is shaping the signal while you work. Hit D to hear it too, soloing the difference between dry and wet so you know precisely what you're adding and taking away.

10

So Much Control

Trim handles input, output, and dry/wet mix. A|B holds two independent states so you can compare a heavier approach against a lighter one. Delta, Undo, Redo, and global bypass are a keystroke away. The Visualizer settings let you tune the room to your taste.

11

Zoom In

The Hz strips run down both walls of the room. Scroll over a strip to zoom in on the part of the spectrum you care about, or back out for the full picture. Hold Shift while you drag for fine gain, or double-click any dB readout to type an exact value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does MIXVAULT do?

MIXVAULT is an immersive EQ that lets you shape your tonal balance by feel. Instead of drawing a curve, you pull frequencies closer to bring them forward and push them away to push them back, in a room that expands and contracts as you work. The bands are set and the Q is optimised for you, so the only thing you touch is the gain.

Isn't a fixed-band EQ limiting?

The opposite. Because the bands don't move, nothing smears. Dragging a frequency or squeezing a Q introduces phase ringing that blurs transients and muddies the low-end, which is why a "bigger" low-end so often ends up sounding softer. MIXVAULT's fixed, phase-stable bands mean a boost is a transparent gain change exactly where you need it. And tonal balance is 95% of EQing anyway.

Can I use MIXVAULT on individual tracks as well as the master?

Yes. MIXVAULT works on channels, busses, and the full mix. Choose Broad for fast, sweeping moves, Balanced for most mixes and masters, Detailed when you need to target something specific, or Bass to focus entirely on the low end.

How do Smart Presets work?

Hit Analyze, tell MIXVAULT your source and genre, and it builds a preset from your actual audio rather than loading a stored curve or an average of a thousand other records. A dark preset for hip hop bass is a completely different shape to a bright preset for modern rock vocals, because it should be. It gets you 80% of the way there, so you spend your energy refining something that already works.
